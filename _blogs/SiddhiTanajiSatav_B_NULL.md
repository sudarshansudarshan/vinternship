When I first started my internship, I was honestly a little clueless about which domain it was going to focus on. Later, when I began exploring the Vibe platform, I realized that the internship was about Full Stack Development. 

At first, I didn’t have much interest in it. But things slowly changed. After attending the stand-ups and the inaugural session, I started noticing the incredible effort the Vicharanashala (Lab for Education Design) team had put into designing this program. 

That really impressed me, and gradually my interest started growing as I kept learning new things. Then came the viva. As a complete beginner, it was quite challenging to remember everything we had learned in just one month. But thankfully, our senior Himanshu P Dev turned the viva into a learning experience rather than something stressful. 

It felt more like a teaching and discussion session led by students. After that, our teams were formed. Personally, the breakout sessions helped me a lot, and I’m really grateful to my group for that. In today’s tech era, many people think coding always needs advanced tools and automation. But while working on the case studies, I realized that if you actually try to write and solve things step by step, you can do it—and that’s where real learning happens.

 Now we are moving towards the project phase, and I’m really excited to build something meaningful and apply everything we’ve learned so far. Grateful for the guidance of Prof. Sudarshan Iyengar sir throughout this internship journey. 

Also thankful to the Pinternship mentor team — Nandan Prabhudesai Sir, Jinal Gupta Mam, Sakshi Sharma Mam, Harshdeep R. Mam, Pavani Mam, Nitin Sankar Sir, Meenakshi V Mam, and Rohit Sharma Sir — for their constant support and encouragement. Special thanks to the Cohort I senior interns Himanshu P Dev, PRATHEEK G, and Ahana Sarkar for sharing their insights.

 Glad to be learning alongside my fellow co-interns and growing together through this experience.
