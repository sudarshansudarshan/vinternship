From my first ever LinkedIn post to finally writing my first blog, this Vinternship has been nothing less than a series of unexpected moments. I never thought I would even share it once, until today’s standup where D Narendran sir talked about how he was also minimally active, but this time he wrote the blog and now I’m here telling my whole story.
 I remember when I was registering for the NPTEL examination, I was confused between choosing Discrete Mathematics or Theory of Computation. I chose Discrete Mathematics because it is a 12 week course, and I thought I could prepare for it after completing my 4 and 8 week exams.

Then, surprisingly, a Vinternship mail popped up. I had mixed emotions at that time, I felt fortunate, but also a bit tensed because it was a big commitment and a great opportunity.

I enrolled in the course, and the very next day, I fell sick. But because the course was so well structured, I found it addictive and still managed to complete my first milestone.

Ramadan was going on at that time, so I used to study till iftar (breaking fast) and continue my progress early in the mornings after sehri. In the last 5 days of Ramadan, due to Eid preparations, I could not progress on ViBe and complete my third milestone. After two days of Eid, the authorities announced the ejection.

I was so tensed, thinking how I would manage to complete the course in one night. I explained my problem to the mentors and requested them to grant me some time to complete it, and they helped me with it. I have finally completed 95% of the course and will soon complete it.

Standups are the best source of motivation to stay consistent in this journey. Anyone who shares their story is an inspiration for others, showing how they manage their academics and the internship. I have learned one strategy from D Narendran sir to solve and submit at least 2 case studies daily, and I am looking forward to implementing that very soon.

Our Discord community is also very helpful. There, everyone competes to help each other, no jealousy, no competition, only pure progress. So many peers conduct breakout sessions to help each other understand and solve the case studies.


From this journey, I have learned that:

> Consistency is the key to success, just like Sudharshan Iyengar sir said in our first standup
> There is a huge difference between just solving a question and actually understanding the concept. I truly felt this in the NPTEL exam hall. My approach to solving problems has improved a lot, all thanks to the Betal (AI generated quizzes) on the ViBe platform
> Time management really matters a lot

I would sincerely like to thank NPTEL , Dr Sudarshan Iyengar sir and VLED Labs for providing this opportunity, Nandan Prabhudesai sir for designing and explaining the course in the best way, and our mentors Rohit Sharma sir, Jinal Gupta ma’am, Nitin Sankar sir, Sakshi Sharma ma’am, for keeping us motivated through such positive and heartwarming standups.
