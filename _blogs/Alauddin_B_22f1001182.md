## title: "My Vinternship Journey"  
author: Md Alauddin Ansari  
vinternship_id: 22f1001182

At first I felt a little under confident but at the same time I was very much sure that the strategy and the structure of this program is definitely going to help me a lot. The key idea that I got from this program is learning by building and it really gave me a good advantage. However real-world problems were so many things and exposure to full stack development so it was really a nice journey learning from this platform.

Challenges came; then again I tried to do the research on topics; then again do some hands-on. Again we are back on the track. Yes it was really good practical growth and a great lesson to learn how we are going to create meaningful technology. It taught many things: the consistency I learnt over perfection and how we can build things under pressure. It was really a very interactive and enjoyable journey and the most part of this program is that I never felt that I'm doing some course or am doing some work. I was just enjoying and that enjoyment was from learning. This is the main take away that I would like to share that I got from this program.
