This internship gave me a lot of knowledge, experience, and friends with whom I can connect and ask my doubts anytime.

Firstly, it was hard for me to understand how I would manage my studies with this internship. But my mind told me one thing: opportunities don’t come again and again. If it comes, grab it. And I did the same.

Slowly, I was able to combine both my studies and internship. It was not easy, but with consistency, I managed it.

The VIBE lectures are very interesting. The structure of the platform is designed in such a way that a person must stay focused. If focus is lost, the score gets minimized, and in some cases, lectures play again. This made me more disciplined and attentive.

Then comes the case study part. It was really good and application-based. In this, we had to develop code related to real-world problems. I had not done this type of work before, so it was a new and interesting experience for me.

The last part is the project section. I am very excited to contribute to a real-world project by applying my knowledge. It feels like I am finally using what I learned in a practical way.

And the most interesting part is the “Health Point Leaderboard.” When I wake up in the morning and before I sleep at night, I always check that leaderboard — at which Position I stand, who is at the top. It motivates me daily. I keep pushing myself, thinking that one day I will be at the top of that leaderboard.

This journey is not just about learning technical skills, but also about discipline, consistency, and self-belief.

Lastly, I want to thank the whole Vinternship team VLED Labs for this opportunity. It really means a lot to me...
