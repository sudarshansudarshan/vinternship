When I received the email for this Vinternship at IIT Ropar, I was honestly surprised.

It was my first internship, and along with excitement, there was a question in my mind—what will this actually be like?

I already had some exposure to development. I had watched tutorials, learned concepts, and had a general understanding of things like React and backend development. So I thought this would be a continuation of that—learning a bit more and moving ahead.

But this experience felt different from the beginning.

As I started the ViBe training, I slowly realized something important:
There is a difference between knowing something and truly understanding it.

Earlier, I could recognize concepts. I knew the terms. I had seen similar code before. But here, I started understanding how things actually work together.

The lectures and problem statements didn’t feel like typical learning material. They felt practical—like situations where I had to think, not just follow steps.
That shift made learning more meaningful.

TypeScript was completely new for me. At first, it felt unfamiliar, but as I continued, I started enjoying it. It added structure to the code and made things feel more clear and organized.

One thing I really appreciated about this internship is how everything is designed. It doesn’t rush me, or tell me what to do.
Instead, it allows me to understand things step by step, in a way that stays with me.
Even the concepts I had seen before started making more sense in this process.

Before this internship, my confidence was uncertain. I knew things, but I wasn’t always sure if I could apply them on my own.

Now, I feel more confident.

Not because I know everything, but because I understand things better—and I trust myself more while solving problems.

This internship didn’t just improve my technical skills. It changed how I look at learning.And I think that is the most valuable thing I’ve gained so far.

As I move forward to the next phase, I’m excited to explore more, build real projects, and continue growing.

A special thanks to Sudarshan Iyengar Sir , Nandan Prabhudesai Sir and VLED Labs for providing such a great opportunity.
