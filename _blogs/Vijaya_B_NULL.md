When I first started the Vinternship in Vicharanashala (Lab for Education Design) , the journey began with something familiar — watching learning videos and solving multiple choice questions based on those topics. This phase helped me revise concepts and test whether I truly understood what I had learned.

At first, it felt like a regular online learning experience. But as the program continued, the process slowly became more interactive and engaging.

After completing the learning modules, we had a viva session conducted by our senior Himanshu P Dev . I would like to thank him for making the session highly interactive and engaging. Initially, I expected it to be a conventional question-and-answer session focused on correctness. However, the experience was quite different.

The viva was not only about answering questions correctly. It became a collaborative discussion where everyone participated. If someone did not know the answer, others would step in and explain the concept. Sometimes we even asked questions to each other based on the areas we wanted to understand better.

After the viva, we moved to the case study phase. These case studies required deeper thinking and practical problem-solving. Instead of simply recalling concepts, we had to apply them and figure out how things actually work.

To facilitate this, we organized ourselves into breakout groups where we shared and discussed our approaches to solving the case studies. Each person explained their logic and the steps they followed to reach a solution. Listening to different perspectives helped me understand how the same problem can be solved in multiple ways.

There were many moments when I got stuck while trying to solve a case study. During those times, my friends helped me a lot. Whenever I was confused, they guided me and explained things patiently. Their support made difficult problems easier to understand.

As we move into the project phase, I look forward to applying my learning to build something meaningful. I am grateful for the guidance of Sudarshan Iyengar , whose support has been valuable throughout this internship journey. The support of other mentors have also helped me during my vinternship journey. So, I am thankful to Nandan Prabhudesai, Jinal Gupta, Sakshi Sharma, Harshdeep R., Nitin Sankar, Meenakshi V, Rohit Sharma, Suruchi Mittal.

Looking back, this Vinternship was not just about completing tasks or learning technical concepts. It was about learning through discussion, collaboration, and consistent practice.

Sometimes learning started with watching a video. Sometimes it happened while solving questions. But many of the most valuable lessons came from conversations — asking doubts, explaining ideas, and helping each other understand better.

This experience reminded me that learning becomes much more meaningful when it is shared.
