# Coding is not my cup of tea..

_March 28, 2026_

---

A little bit about me. I did my undergraduate studies in Electrical & Electronics Engineering, followed by a PhD in Materials Science & Engineering. I currently work full-time—and I’m also a mother to a wonderful 3-year-old daughter.

For most of my academic life, I stayed away from coding and computer science—even when it was the most sought-after field. I always believed in hard sciences and mathematics. I told myself, _“Coding is not my cup of tea.”_

I wanted to solve real-world problems by getting my hands dirty, not by sitting behind a screen. In fact, until very recently, I didn’t fully believe in simulations as a precursor to scientific discovery.

---

## What Changed?

One small moment. A colleague of mine used Python to solve a real problem that had been troubling my team for quite some time. Watching that unfold sparked something in me. Around the same time, I came across courses in Data Science and AI—yes, the buzzwords. Curious, I enrolled in a course taught by Prof. **Sudarshan Iyengar** and learned the fundamentals of Python.

_That small spark turned into a fire._

Even after that, I kept coding at a safe distance. Python just became a tool—something I used to automate parts of my work. But I wasn’t ready to fully step into the world of software development. Then one day, I received an email.

A **2-month workshop** promising to teach full-stack development using the MERN stack. My first reaction? _“That’s impossible.”_ But then I thought—_why not try?_ Especially when it was offered by Prof. Sudarshan sir. So, I applied. And I got in.

---

## Six Weeks Later... A Different Story

I’m now six weeks into the program. Do I claim to know MERN well? Not at all. But the way we learn here is something else—it’s addictive and exhilarating. Within the first two weeks, I completed all the video lectures. I picked up some TypeScript, and I jokingly say I know a bit of React (though I’m still not entirely convinced 😄). But what truly impressed me were MongoDB and Express. There’s something incredibly satisfying about building systems end-to-end.

Did I stop there? Not even close. I teamed up with three incredibly driven individuals (Rytham, Kavya and Atharva), and we signed up for an internal hackathon. From someone who once avoided coding... to someone voluntarily joining a hackathon?! That’s a transformation I never imagined for myself.

My days are full—with work, responsibilities, and motherhood. But every night, after my little angel falls asleep, I spend two hours learning. Working on milestones. Solving case studies. Building projects. Those quiet late-night hours have become my most meaningful investment in myself.

---

This journey wouldn’t have been possible without the incredible ecosystem behind it. A heartfelt thanks to **VLED Labs**, Prof. **Sudarshan Iyengar**, **Rohit Sharma**, **Jinal Gupta**, **Riya Mehta**, **Nitin Sankar**, and many others working behind the scenes.

From a student who avoided coding... to someone who now looks forward to building things—This has been a transformation I never saw coming.

And I’m just getting started.
