Even after securing a Gold in *The Joy of Computing*, I initially missed the opportunity to be part of the Vinternship program. It was disappointing at first, but a few days later, I got another chance to apply—and that changed everything.

That moment marked the beginning of my journey with VLED.

## 🚀 Beginning of the Journey
I started the program with a lot of excitement and curiosity. Like many learning journeys, the beginning felt energetic and fast-paced. However, as I progressed, there was a slight drop in momentum. This made me realize that consistency and depth of understanding matter far more than just completing tasks.

## 🎯 Key Learning Experience
One of the most valuable parts of this experience was the viva session with Himanshu P Dev. It was not just an evaluation, but a meaningful discussion that helped me clarify concepts and strengthen my fundamentals. That interaction significantly influenced how I approached the rest of the program.

## 💡 Case Studies & Practical Learning
The case studies played a crucial role in my learning. They pushed me to think practically, apply concepts, and gain insights before stepping into the project phase. This transition from theory to application was one of the most impactful aspects of the internship.

## 📈 Overall Growth
Overall, this experience has been a strong learning curve—filled with both highs and moments of reflection. It has helped me grow not only academically but also in the way I approach learning itself.

## 🙏 Acknowledgment
I would like to express my sincere gratitude to Prof. Sudarshan Iyengar, the Vicharanashala (Lab for Education Design) team, and mentors such as Meenakshi V, Jinal Gupta, Nandan Prabhudesai, and Sakshi Sharma for their continuous guidance and support throughout this journey.

I am also grateful to the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar and Annam.AI for providing this opportunity. This internship taught me lessons that go far beyond the course itself.
