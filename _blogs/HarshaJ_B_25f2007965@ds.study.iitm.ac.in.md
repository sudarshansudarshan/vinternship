I started my Vinternship on February 14th, 2026, and successfully wrapped up the ViBe learning phase on March 25th. What an incredible journey it’s been!

Scaling the learning curve from JavaScript and TypeScript to React, MongoDB, and Express.js was intense. The volume of content was heavy, and adapting to the structured, proctored environment of the ViBe platform was a challenge at first. However, it was exactly what I needed to build real discipline and consistency.

The case studies were a game-changer. They ensured I wasn’t just passively consuming content, but actively applying concepts and staying on the right track. Paired with the HP points system, my motivation and engagement stayed high throughout the program.

A huge shoutout to the Discord community. Whenever I hit a roadblock (which was often!), my peers were always there to clarify doubts. Our daily standups acted as great icebreakers, and the breakout rooms became brilliant collaborative spaces where we tackled complex concepts together.

I want to extend my sincere gratitude to Sudarshan Iyengar Sir and VLED Labs , ANNAM.AI , Indian Institute of Technology, Ropar  for providing this wonderful opportunity. This experience has been incredibly enriching, and I’m genuinely proud of my growth. I'm looking forward to applying these skills and building even more! 📈
