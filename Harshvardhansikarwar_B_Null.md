A few months ago, I was completely stuck. If you’ve ever Googled a "Web Development Roadmap," you know exactly how it feels. MERN, Python , and everyone shouting about AI and Web3. I felt like I was trying to learn everything at once, but the reality was that I was learning nothing.

Cutting Through the Noise

The training phase on ViBe was a bit of a reality check. I realized my confusion didn’t actually come from not knowing enough framework, it came from not having a solid foundation.

The hours spent sitting in front of laptop with attention for the video to count, the quizzes ensuring strong fundamentals and drive to keep hp points safe while also learning the basics.

Real Problems > Tutorials

The biggest shift for me happened during the Case Studies. There is a massive difference between following a smooth YouTube tutorial and handling real world problems. There were times I was frustrated when things broke, and the feedback from the mentors was always very direct. But looking back, those were the moments where I actually grew.

What Stays With Me

The most important thing I’m taking away from this internship isn’t just a certificate or a few new lines on my resume. It’s a sense of direction. To anyone else feeling overwhelmed by the endless options out there: stop looking at the hundred different paths and just focus on building a strong base. Everything else gets a lot easier after that.
