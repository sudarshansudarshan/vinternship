This video provides an overview of the Vinternship at VLED Lab, including its structure, learning approach, and workflow. It highlights how students engage in practical problem-solving, apply technical concepts, and develop skills through hands-on experience along with finding solutions to problems with others who are doing the internship. Plus one gets to do some interesting activities while learning. 

Viewers will understand what Vinternship is and what they can expect from the program.
