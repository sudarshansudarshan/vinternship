This video presents my experience during the Vinternship program conducted by the Vicharanshala Lab at Indian Institute of Technology Ropar. 
In this vlog, I briefly discuss my learning journey through the internship case studies and the insights gained from working on them.
During the internship, I also took the initiative to form and coordinate a small team of around 6–7 participants to collaboratively explore and discuss different case studies. 
One of the most interesting learnings came from the 9th case study on the concept of type alias, where I explored ideas and perspectives that go beyond typical academic learning.
Overall, this experience helped me develop a deeper understanding of concepts, collaborative learning, and the importance of reflecting on knowledge beyond the boundaries of regular coursework.
